Data Controller

Otto is operated and hosted by its developer. All data is processed and controlled by the bot operator.

Data Collected

  • User IDs, Server IDs, Channel IDs
  • Moderation records (warnings, bans, timeouts)
  • Appeal submissions and outcomes

Purpose of Data

Data is used strictly to provide moderation functionality, logging, and appeal processing.

Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for moderation and operational purposes.
